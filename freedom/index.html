What is a freedom number?

Your freedom number is the amount of money you need invested to live off returns and stop trading time for money. This is also what a lot of people think about with retirement. Your number depends on the life you actually want, where you live, and how much of a buffer you want in the future. Two minutes from now, you'll know yours.

Knowing your freedom number can significantly change the actions you take for the rest of your life if you're serious about it.

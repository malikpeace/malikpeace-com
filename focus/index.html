What is this?

This is a free area I made to help people focus.

Over time I've realized that focus is by FAR the most important thing when it comes to getting things done. And it helps me a lot to turn off everything, set a timer for 3-5 hours and just lose myself in whatever I'm doing. As I find myself accomplishing more in 1 focused hour than 10 distracted hours.

So I made this to help. I made it very very simple but I hope it's useful and the results you get from it are tangible and beneficial to your life.

What is this?

This is a simple tool here to help you visualize how much time you have left and how much of it goes to waste.

Time is arguably the most important thing you have.

Most of us live our entire lives rarely thinking about how much time we have and how we spend it. Mostly because we're busy spending that time distracted, but also because it's incredibly scary to think about what happens when the time runs out.

The goal is for you to understand how much or how little time you have, and where it's going, so you're at least aware to make changes if needed.

"A man has two lives. The second begins when he realizes he only has one."Confucius

Anyways, I hope this is useful to you. Enjoy :)

    So how do you fix this?

    The obvious answer is to just get off your phone. Which is useful, but that alone simply doesn't work for a lot of people long term. Because if you put the phone down and have nothing better to do, you're gonna pick it right back up. Because your brain is no longer getting stimulation/reward from anything. And in my experience, the best thing to do to fix this, is to find something you care about more than anything else, that just the progress towards that goal feels more rewarding than anything your phone can give you.

    1. Set a daily screen time target

    0years back
    0months back
    0weeks back

    2. Replace it with something you actually care about

    A lot of people just remove but never replace. But if there's nothing on the other side, nothing important to make progress towards when you put your phone down, your brain goes right back to the phone because it's the easiest reward available.

    To fix this, you simply need to make progress towards a goal you ACTUALLY care about. Once you can see yourself getting closer to something that matters to you, scrolling starts to feel repulsive to your brain.

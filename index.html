If you are reading this, I assume you want to get more out of your life.(good!) And when you research a million different people on how to do this, you’ll get a million different answers.

But the common denominator between ALL of them is that they took:

Consistentfocusedaction.

Please if you can, staple this to the front of your forehead or have it posted up on a wall somewhere. Because that right there, is basically the secret to success. Literally, almost 90% of all motivational self improvement whatever, can be summed up with just those 3 words.

Seriously, look at anyone great in any area, and 99/100 you’ll find relentless, unwavering, almost obsessive focus for a part of their life.

  1. Isaac NewtonWorked out the beginnings of calculus in his early 20s, in 1665 and 1666, while Cambridge was closed for the plague.
  2. Leonardo da VinciStarted the Mona Lisa around 1503 and kept refining it for years, possibly until 1517.
  3. Charles DarwinSpent more than 20 years gathering evidence before publishing On the Origin of Species in 1859.
  4. Albert EinsteinWrote four breakthrough papers in 1905, including special relativity, while working full time at a patent office.
  5. Mahatma GandhiWalked about 240 miles in 24 days on the 1930 Salt March.
  6. Serena WilliamsTrained with her dad on public courts in Compton as a kid, then won 23 Grand Slam singles titles.
  7. Steve JobsCo-founded Apple in 1976, was forced out in 1985, came back in 1997, and introduced the iPhone 10 years later.
  8. J.K. RowlingThe first Harry Potter book was turned down by 12 publishers before Bloomsbury said yes in 1996.
  9. Kobe BryantKnown for training before sunrise. He called his relentless approach the Mamba Mentality, and wrote a book with that name.
  10. MrBeastUploaded for about 5 years with little success before “I Counted to 100,000!” took off in 2017.
  11. Malik PeaceMy first videos got 30 views, and half of them were me on my iPad. For years it barely grew. I kept going, got better, and now 65.9k people watch.

But to make it more tangible for you, this is how to actually lock in:

  1. Stare at your wall or yourself in your eyes in the mirror and think about your existence. Doesn’t matter if it’s good or bad just listen. Thinking is not, watching a video and thinking. It’s not having music in the background and thinking. It’s very simple to just say “think about your life,” but it’s one of the most uncomfortable things any human can do, but necessary if you wanna get the most out of your existence. You can never expect to fix or change your life if you don’t do this.

  2. You are a biological machine. If you sit in the dark, eat bullshit, barely sleep, don’t move, what do you expect is going to happen. First Sleep. I think this is.. probably the most important one of the three. Here’s a quick little tip, if you can, go to bed around 10pm, or at the very least put your phone down at 10pm. Doing the physical things gives you energy. And not doing stupid shit gives you focus, to put that energy on the right things. Also before I get into the next part, the base doesn’t have to be perfect right. Like you don’t need to be 100% with this. Even 80% 50% of the time is better than zero.

  3. What we’re climbing towards. It’s your purpose, your philosophy, the reason you get up. But past that, we need something bigger, a mountain to climb that will satisfy us. A mountain that we choose to climb. But if you stay at the base long enough, even if you don’t realize it, you will climb, but the climb will be in the benefit of someone else and you will only realize at the end, you were helping someone else climb their mountain without discovering your own.

  4. I had a video game addiction. So to fix this problem… I sold my PS5. And I’ll tell you, it’s very hard to play Tekken at 2am if you don’t have a tv or PS5 in your house. Delete TikTok. Delete Instagram. Delete apps you know are adding nothing to your life. Moderation doesn’t work. I highly recommend, DELETE, BUILD, then SLOWLY add if you can manage it and want it back.

  5. To start to focus a lot of the time you just need to force yourself to start, keep going for a bit, maybe 5-15 minutes, then your brain will automatically focus. It’s when you do nothing but ONE thing. It’s when you’re uninterrupted in a clear, quiet area, no phone, I like to listen to music with no lyrics, some caffeine, maybe earlier in the morning if you like that, just locked in on ONE goal.

  6. CONSISTENCY BEATS INTENSITY. 3/10 effort for 3 years is better than 9/10 effort for 3 weeks. First thing I try to remember this thing I wrote down years ago: when you fall down, which will happen, the speed in which you can get back up will determine your life. 1st, ask yourself and be honest, do you need to stop, take a break and chill? 2nd, check your vitals. What I mean by that is, sleep, diet, exercise.

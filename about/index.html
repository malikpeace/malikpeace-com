Growing up

Hello, my name is Malik Peace. You most likely know me from YouTube. I was born in 2001. I grew up playing V.Smile and Tekken 3 on a PS1, trading Silly Bandz, and staying up late reading Percy Jackson, which were the first books I actually enjoyed reading on my own.

My profile picture is me at 5 years old. That kid loved space, the stars and making art. He wanted to be an astrophysicist and got in trouble sometimes for talking too much. He would literally walk on stage and do magic tricks in front of the whole school. He wasn’t scared of anything. I’m not saying I’m perfect, but now, I think he’s smiling at me again.

18 and 19

At 18 and 19 I was consuming 6 to 10 hours a day of self improvement content. Books, podcasts, videos, whatever. I wasn’t really taking action yet. But honestly that time changed my life, because knowledge is power.

The first piece of power I ever learned is that I’m in control of my life, there are no rules, and I can change my future based on the actions I take in the present.

Trying to get rich

Around 19 I got obsessed with money. I read hella books on money and mindset, bought a course, and made a dropshipping store. Then I bought cheap watches and tried to start a luxury watch brand called Amripeace. I even bought the domain lol. Didn’t go anywhere. Then a clothing brand just called Peace. Even this channel started with me talking about money and finance, hoping to sell some courses and get rich in a few months. But I stopped, because I realized I was being stupid.

The first time I felt alive

One day in college I was just chillin, and for no real reason (prolly cuz I was sick of my life) I turned off my phone and sat in silence for a few minutes. I wasn’t focused on anything. And I realized that at any moment I can just stop and take in the world. You ever look at something and it just hits you, like “woah... I’m actually alive”? That was the first time I ever felt that.

YouTube

Early on I would post a video and it would get like 30 views, and half of them were me on my iPad on other accounts lol. At 143 subscribers it was so easy to just do nothing. Years went by and I only had a couple hundred subscribers. It felt pointless. But I kept going, got better, and my videos started getting decent. 50,000 subscribers was a massive goal that felt almost impossible. It’s 65.9k now.

Making videos has a lot of boring, down moments. Whenever I feel like stopping, I think about the people on the other side of the screen who will actually get something out of it if I keep going. So “editing clips for this next video” turns into “helping someone see the world differently.”

Now

I’m halfway through my 20s now. I’ve spent a lot of them trying to build my dream, and a lot of them wasting time, like most people do. I haven’t hit every goal I set 5 years ago, but I’ve made way more progress than I ever thought I would. The plan was always to make progress in my 20s and really start hitting my big goals in my 30s. So right now I’m making videos and building Memento.

How I make videos

  • I write and record a lot, for YouTube and other stuff.
  • My setup is an M4 Mac mini and a bunch of keyboards. I got into custom mechanical keyboards years ago.
  • My phone is basically a smart brick I use to change my lights and play music. I deleted the YouTube app and only kept YouTube Studio.
  • My best days: early in the morning, no food in my stomach, just coffee and water, and a stopwatch going on my Apple Watch.
  • How a video gets made: come up with the idea, write the script, record it, edit the audio, add clips, then edit everything else.
  • Simple editing is better. At least for me.

25 lessons I learned at 25

I made a whole video on this. Here they are real quick.

  1. Comfort kills you slowlyComfort beyond a point begins to do more harm than good.
  2. Time flies, but it’s also incredibly slowIf someone asks what you’ve been up to this past year and it’s only a sentence, fix that.
  3. 99.99% of people don’t think about youPut yourself first. Think for yourself and stop letting other people’s opinions run your life.
  4. You are going to suffer no matter whatNo matter what path you take it’s going to be hard. Why not take the path that gives you the most at the end.
  5. Think. Think. Think about your lifeUse your brain, it’s the most complex thing in the universe.
  6. Drinking is overratedYou just feel tired and awake at the same time.
  7. 95% of things don’t matter nearly as much as you thinkIf you care about everything you care about nothing.
  8. Music can change your state within secondsUse music as a supplement to life.
  9. Drink a gallon of water every dayThere’s zero downside to doing this.
  10. Learn to be boredYou adapt to boredom a lot faster than you think.
  11. Focus on yourself so you have something to offerJust make sure you’re good and stable first.
  12. The internet can drain your life or build itIf you’re going to spend 10 hours a day looking at a screen, get something from it.
  13. Compare yourself to others*Just make sure it’s useful and you gain something.
  14. Sleep and diet affect mood, energy and clarity more than anythingYou’re a biological machine, take care of yourself.
  15. Question everythingLearn something from everyone. Not everything from anyone.
  16. Talk to strangersGenuinely become interested in other people.
  17. Your morning dictates the rest of your dayIf you have a good morning, the rest of the day becomes 5 times easier.
  18. Break the cycle todayStarting is the perfect condition.
  19. Be kind to others no matter whatPeople at the end of the day are trying their best.
  20. No better feeling than progress toward a worthy goalProgress towards a worthy goal is what the human mind feeds off of.
  21. Speaking negatively to yourself does nothingDo not shoot yourself down before taking off.
  22. Think about your death often, but don’t obsess over itWe’re here now, that’s something, at least enjoy it.
  23. Learn, but also take actionOtherwise it’s only progress in loading the spring, not launching it.
  24. You can feel emotions without having to act on themFear is a mile wide but an inch deep.
  25. Take your life seriouslyAction x consistency = results.
